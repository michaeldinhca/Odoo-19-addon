Enterprise Remittance Management

Know exactly what every vendor payment settled, and tell the vendor.

Immutable allocation history

Every vendor payment made from the Register Payment wizard keeps a relational record of the bills and credits it settled: original amount, amount due before payment, applied amount, remaining immediately after payment, in the document currency. Recorded before reconciliation, from the wizard's own mapping, so a credit offset can never hide a bill.

Remittance slip and email

A native PDF report bound to payments and a vendor email with the slip attached. Bills and credits in separate tables, credits negative, balances remaining highlighted, one currency label per amount.

Safe reset, cancel and delete

Keep the bill/credit offsets or reverse only the ones this registration created and that are still safe to reverse. Lock dates, access rights and companies are honoured; every decision is logged.

Optional Studio migration

Import legacy allocation data with a dry run, a full issue report and no invented values.
